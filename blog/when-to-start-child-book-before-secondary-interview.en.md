## When do DSS and private secondary interviews usually start?

What families often see is this: some DSS and private secondaries already hold a briefing or open applications in Primary 5, and the busier interview stretch falls in Primary 6 first term, the first half of the school year before Secondary 1. Some schools are earlier, some are later, and some mainly meet families after an application or an interview letter. This is a way to line up the year. It does not mean any one school follows this calendar, and it is not a single date for aided discretionary places. ## If the family starts at the beginning of Primary 4, how do the five stages meet Primary 6 first term?

Take “around September of Primary 4” as an example. It is not a promise that every family prints on the same day. Month 1 is the consultation: age, whether the target is DSS, private, or another secondary, and one interest the child will open and work on. Months 2 to 5 are mentor-led making. Months 6 to 7 are the publishing discussion, and a university professor may review the work. That review is part of publishing. It is not a school reference. Months 8 to 10 are parent-and-child layout. Month 11 is a check before print. The published phases do not turn that month into another course. Month 12 is the formal print run, and the start of a physical bookstore listing path for that title and package. Listing is a distributor supply path. The store buyer and the store manager decide stock, display, and returns. Month 12 does not mean every branch has a copy that day, and an ISBN is not a shelf. Kidsmybook is not a bookstore chain. - Months 2 to 5: mentor-led making, with notes on what the child observed and changed.
- Months 6 to 7: publishing discussion. A university professor may review.
- Months 8 to 10: parent-and-child layout, keeping the child's own sentences.

- Month 12, around the time before Primary 5 begins: formal print, and the start of a bookstore-platform listing path.

## How does one ISBN children's book sit in a secondary personal learning portfolio and a Clear Book?

Put it at the front of the personal learning portfolio, and at the front of the clear display book families carry to the interview (often called a Clear Book), as one finished project, not between certificates. The book does not replace school reports, and it does not replace a stack of certificates. Reports are the academic record. Certificates show which classes the child attended. An ISBN book the child helped make shows something else: an interest kept up over time, and a publication someone can cite — the title, the child's part, the year, and whether a university professor reviewed it. Secondary, DSS, and direct-subsidy interviews more often ask about a sustained interest and a short account in the student's own words. One sheet names the page to open. Certificates and tutoring work stay behind it. The portfolio is still the second use. Publishing plus a physical bookstore listing comes first. ## If the family is only starting in Primary 6, should they still rush a book?

If the interview is only days or weeks away, the 12-month plan will not finish for this round. This page will not ask a family to sign up and rush a print run. Narrow the papers already in hand, and choose one piece the child can explain. A book fits the next cycle that still has about a year. It does not rescue a round that is already this close.
