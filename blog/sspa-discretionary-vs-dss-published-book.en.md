Parents who search for Secondary One discretionary places often finish the thought with “government and aided Primary One places do not ask for a portfolio, so secondary does not need a book either”, or “hand the ISBN in for central allocation”. This page does not start from a binder, and it does not repeat a DSS interview-skills article. The Kidsmybook lead is publishing plus a physical bookstore listing: the child takes part, an about-12-month mentored programme prints the book, and the title and package then follow a Hong Kong physical bookstore listing / distribution path. A school file can cite that result. It is a second use. It is not the first sentence of the plan, and it is not a school place.

Three secondary routes get mixed together. The first is Discretionary Places under the Secondary School Places Allocation system, often called SSPA, at participating schools. Those schools may arrange an interview. The Education Bureau’s public note for this stage is that participating schools must not conduct written tests. The second is Central Allocation under the same system. A computer processes the allocation band, the family’s school choices, and the school net. That result itself has no interview. The third is a DSS, private, or international school running its own admission. A briefing, an application, and an interview are set by the school. Some of those schools take a thin file, and the day may allow a physical object. Government and aided Primary One discretionary places are a different system. ## Are Secondary One discretionary places the same as a no-portfolio round?

No. At schools taking part in Secondary School Places Allocation, the discretionary-places stage can include an interview. The Education Bureau’s public note for this stage is that participating schools must not conduct written tests. Each school publishes its admission criteria in advance. The Education Bureau provides the school with the applicants’ rank order. How much weight goes to the interview, conduct, extra-curricular activities, or other items follows that school’s notice for that year. The list of participating schools, the dates, and the notes follow that year’s Education Bureau release. Whether a DSS school joins the system also follows that year’s list and that school’s own notice.

A formal publication is not a document every participating school asks for, and it does not rewrite the rank order the Education Bureau provides. If that school’s criteria include an interview, and the day allows talk about an interest or an achievement, or allows a physical object, the child can describe one finished interest. If the day allows the book, point to one page. If the school follows only its published criteria, does not discuss activities that day, and takes no extra file, do not add a binder the school did not ask for. Do not photocopy the whole book into the application. The secondary page: https://kidsmybook.com/secondary-dss-admissions-portfolio .

## Will bringing a book change a central-allocation result?

No. Central allocation is processed by computer. The allocation band comes from internal assessments after the Education Bureau’s scaling, together with the family’s choices and the school net. That computer result has no interview, and it has no slot for a portfolio. A formal publication does not enter that calculation. Choice strategy, ties within a band, and school-net detail follow that year’s Education Bureau notes.

If, after the central-allocation result, a family separately asks a school whether a place is still open, that is another scene. It is not the computer result, and it is not the discretionary-places stage. Whether anyone meets the family, and whether a file is accepted, is that school’s choice on the day.

## At DSS, private, and international schools, which scenes are worth a published book?

When that school runs its own admission, takes a thin file, or the interview asks about a sustained interest and allows the child to show or talk about one finished project. A DSS school that does not join Secondary School Places Allocation sets its own admission. A briefing, an application, and an interview are common. Private and international schools likewise do not use the central-allocation computer result. The page count, whether a portfolio is accepted, whether a physical object may be carried in, and whether the school runs its own assessment, follow that school’s latest guidelines.

That is the scene where a child-involved ISBN publication is most often useful. The front of the file can hold one page: the title, the child’s part, the ISBN, and an optional line on the Hong Kong physical bookstore listing / distribution path. That one page can say the following, still inside the school’s latest form:

- The book title
- What the child did: where the interest came from, the child’s part, and which page they changed
- The ISBN, which identifies this version
- An optional line: formally printed, with a Hong Kong physical bookstore listing / distribution path for this title and package; the store decides stock, display, and returns

Do not put a branch guarantee, an offer of a place, or a price on that page. If the day allows an object, bring the book, point to one page while speaking, and do not read from the cover to the last page.

## Can a published book guarantee a school place?

No. Kidsmybook is a publishing studio. It is not an admissions agent, it has no school partnership, and it does not represent any school in Secondary School Places Allocation or any DSS, private, or international school. The school decides. Central allocation follows that year’s procedure and the computer. Discretionary places follow that school’s published criteria and the Education Bureau notes. In a scene that actually allows the telling or the object, a book can give the conversation one publication someone can check. It is a portfolio story the child can finish and show. It does not replace the rank order, and it does not replace the computer allocation.

## If discretionary places or an interview are only days away, should a family open a new book in order to submit a file?

If the file is only being thought of now, the 12-month plan will not finish for this round, and a Hong Kong physical bookstore listing / distribution path will not appear in those days either. Central allocation does not take the book, so there is nothing to rush-print for that result. If a discretionary-place interview, or a DSS, private, or international interview, is only days or weeks away, do not rush a weak book into a binder either.
