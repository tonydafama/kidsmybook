Parents who hear “available online” may be looking at a product page they can order, an ebook catalog they can download, or a belief that a Hong Kong physical bookstore already has the book on a shelf. All three can be called a listing. What a parent or an interviewer can check is different. This page only separates those three. The Kidsmybook lead is publishing plus a physical bookstore listing path. An online link is not the finish line.

## What are the three meanings of “listed”?

The first is a product page on an online shop: Amazon, HKTVmall, or a print-on-demand web shop, where someone places an order and a copy is then printed or sent from a warehouse. The second is an ebook-store catalog: a reader downloads or reads on a screen, and may never hold a paper book. The third is a physical store shelf: a Hong Kong physical bookstore. After a distributor supplies the title, the store decides whether to stock it, where to display it, and whether to return it. For parents and for an admissions interview, the third meaning adds two things someone can check: a book a person can hold and open, and a buyer or store manager who actually took stock in. A web page or a download shows that the title can be found online. A stock-in shows that someone agreed to put that book on their own shelf. Kidsmybook is not a bookstore chain. ## Why is “available on Amazon, HKTVmall, or a POD web shop” not stock at Hong Kong physical bookstores?

An online listing shows that a shop will host a product page, or that a print-on-demand system will accept an order. A physical shelf is another path. After a formal print run, a distributor supplies the book. The store buyer and the store manager then decide stock, display, and returns. A title that sells online does not become stock in Hong Kong physical bookstore branches. No branch is guaranteed to have a copy. Kidsmybook is not a bookstore chain. ## Does an ISBN equal a physical bookstore listing?

No. An ISBN identifies one version. A book number shows that a version was registered. It does not show that any bookstore has ordered the title, and it does not turn an online page into a shelf. ## What three checks come before a private quotation?

Prices are not on the site. Before a quotation, a family can ask three questions. Is the offer online-only, a web shop or an ebook? Is there a Hong Kong physical bookstore listing / distribution path, or only a web-shop link? Does anyone claim that every branch is guaranteed to stock the book? The honest answer is that no branch can be guaranteed. The store decides stock, display, and returns. Ask those three, then use WhatsApp or email.
