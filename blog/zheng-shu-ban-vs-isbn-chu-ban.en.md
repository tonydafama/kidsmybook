## What is the portfolio difference between a certificate class and an ISBN book?

A certificate class — a grade exam, a weekend specialist course, a completion slip — usually produces one sheet: the course name, the level, and the date. It shows enrolment and a grade. A formal ISBN publication is a physical book: a topic the child took part in, a page they can point to, and a formal publishing path. In a few minutes, another new grade sheet looks like the file before it. A book someone can open is easier to remember.

## Are certificate classes useful?

Yes. A grade can be a record of practice, especially when the child really trained. The problem is not that certificates have no value.
