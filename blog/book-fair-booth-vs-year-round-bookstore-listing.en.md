Parents who hear “published at the book fair” may be looking at a stall: a publisher or organiser table at the Hong Kong Book Fair, or at another fair, where someone walking past can see the book. Or they may be looking at something else: a formally printed book that then goes toward a Hong Kong physical bookstore listing / distribution path, where the store decides whether to stock it. Both sentences can mean “the book was seen”. The time and the place are different. A fair is a booth for a set of days. A year-round bookstore listing is a supply path. This page only separates those two.

## Does a book at a fair booth mean it is in bookstores all year?

No. A fair booth or a publisher’s stall shows that the book could be seen in that hall for those days. When the fair ends, the stall comes down. Appearing at a fair does not leave a copy on the shelves of Hong Kong physical bookstore branches for the rest of the year. A year-round listing is a supply arrangement with the bookstore platform. The people who buy for the chain, and the store manager, decide stock, display, and returns. Kidsmybook is not a bookstore chain. No branch is guaranteed to have a copy. ## Does “published at the book fair” from self-pay, POD, or a memorial album mean a year-round shelf?

No. Self-funded printing, print-on-demand, and memorial albums often sell “published at the book fair”. That usually means a stall during the fair, or a batch of books an organiser takes to the hall. It can be a family keepsake. It can be one public appearance. It is not a path onto Hong Kong physical bookstore shelves for the rest of the year. The Kidsmybook lead is publishing plus a listing path: about 12 months of mentoring, a formally printed book the child takes part in, then a bookstore-platform arrangement that depends on the title and the package. The measure of success is not “there was a fair booth”, and it is not “there is an ISBN”. ## Do an ISBN and book registration equal a bookstore listing?

No. An ISBN identifies one version. Hong Kong book registration and legal deposit mean copies are delivered and recorded with the Books Registration Office. Details follow the Hong Kong Public Libraries / Books Registration Office. Kidsmybook is not that office. A book number, and a deposited copy, show that a version was registered. They do not show that any bookstore has ordered the title. Why an ISBN is not a bookstore shelf: https://kidsmybook.com/blog/isbn-is-not-bookstore-listing .
