## When do DSS and private primary interviews usually start?

What families often see is this: some first rounds begin in late K2, and the busier stretch falls in K3 first term, the first half of the school year before Primary 1. Some schools are earlier, some are later, and some mainly meet families after a door-knocking visit or an interview letter. This is a way to line up the year. It does not mean any one school follows this calendar. The primary interview page: https://kidsmybook.com/primary-one-interview-portfolio .

## Which grade should a family start if they want formal print and a physical bookstore listing?

If the finish line is a formally printed book plus a listing path toward a Hong Kong physical bookstore listing / distribution path, and a digital file is not enough, allow about 12 months. Against a typical K3 first-term peak, the closer start is the beginning of K2. Count about a year from the start of K2, and month 12 falls around the summer before K3 begins. That is when a printed book, and the start of a listing path, can exist before that peak. If the schools a family cares about already interview in late K2, the 12 months need to begin earlier, around late K1. A start in K3 can still serve later school visits, or the next cycle that still has about a year. It will not finish a book for a round that is only weeks away. ## If the family starts at the beginning of K2, how do the five stages meet K3 first term?

Take “around September of K2” as an example. It is not a promise that every family prints on the same day. Month 1 is the consultation: age, whether the target is DSS, private, or another primary, and one interest the child will open and work on. Months 2 to 5 are mentor-led making. Months 6 to 7 are the publishing discussion, and a university professor may review the work. That review is part of publishing. It is not a school reference. Months 8 to 10 are parent-and-child layout. Month 11 is a check before print. The published phases do not turn that month into another course. Month 12 is the formal print run, and the start of a physical bookstore listing path for that title and package. Listing is a distributor supply path. The store buyer and the store manager decide stock, display, and returns. Month 12 does not mean every branch has a copy that day, and an ISBN is not a shelf. Kidsmybook is not a bookstore chain. The only named public case is Hilary, who was eight and spent about a year on a butterfly guide. That is a publishing timeline, not a kindergarten interview result, and not an offer: https://kidsmybook.com/case-studies/hilary-butterfly-guide .

- Month 1, near the start of K2: consultation. Set the interest and the primary direction.
- Months 2 to 5: mentor-led making, with notes on what the child observed and changed.
- Months 6 to 7: publishing discussion. A university professor may review.
- Months 8 to 10: parent-and-child layout, keeping the child's own sentences.
- Month 11: a check before print.
- Month 12, around the time before K3 begins: formal print, and the start of a bookstore-platform listing path.

## If the interview is already close, should the family still start a book?

If the interview or a door-knocking visit is only days or weeks away, the 12-month plan will not finish in time. This page will not ask a family to sign up and rush a print run. Narrow the papers already in hand, and choose one piece the child can explain. A short interview class can still practise how the child speaks in the room.
