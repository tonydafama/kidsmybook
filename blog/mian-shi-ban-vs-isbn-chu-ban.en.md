## What is the difference between a primary interview class and an ISBN portfolio?

A primary interview class is usually short: manners, a self-introduction, common questions, and a mock interview. What it produces is practice and how the child handles the room. An ISBN portfolio produces a physical book: a topic the child took part in, a page they can point to, and a formal publishing path. In a few minutes, another mock answer can sound like the family before. A book someone can open is easier to remember. Primary page: https://kidsmybook.com/sheng-xiao-xue-mian-shi-zuo-pin-ji .
