After the Hong Kong Primary 1 central allocation results are released, if the outcome isn't ideal, parents enter the fierce "door-knocking" phase. During this time, schools receive mountains of portfolios. Principals and directors might only spend a few dozen seconds on each resume.

Why should they stop and look at yours?

## The 3 Fatal Flaws of Door-Knocking Portfolios

1. **Too Thick and Cluttered**: Dozens of pages of photocopied certificates with no clear focus. Principals simply don't have time to read them.
2. **Lack of Narrative**: Only cold scores and grades, revealing nothing about the child's personality.
3. **Cookie-Cutter**: Everyone has recitation, drawing, and piano. It fails to leave a lasting impression.

## The "Ultimate Weapon" to Catch a Principal's Eye

At the critical moment of door-knocking, you need a **"Highlight"** that instantly grabs attention. This is why compiling a child's work into a **formally published physical book** offers an overwhelming advantage.

### 1. Visual Impact and Professionalism
When other parents hand over a plastic folder, you hand over a hardcover physical book with an ISBN barcode and perhaps even a recommendation from a university professor. This level of professionalism and dedication immediately shows the school how much your family values education.

### 2. Proving Comprehensive Abilities
Publishing a book is more than just writing or drawing; it proves the child possesses:
- **Long-term Focus**: The ability to persevere through a months-long project.
- **Logical Thinking**: The ability to organize scattered thoughts into coherent content.
- **Self-Expression**: The courage to share their views with the world.

### 3. Providing Excellent Interview Topics
Door-knocking interviews are short. If the child can proactively introduce their book to the principal, it not only eases tension but allows them to control the pace of the interview and show confidence.

## Conclusion

Door-knocking is a battlefield. Instead of struggling in traditional extracurricular classes, take a different path. Use a dedicated physical book to open the doors of elite schools for your child.
