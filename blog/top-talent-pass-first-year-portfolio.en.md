Families who have just arrived in Hong Kong via the Top Talent Pass or Quality Migrant schemes often feel anxious about the fierce local school admissions competition. To integrate quickly, many parents' first reaction is to **frantically enroll their children in various extracurricular classes**.

But this is often the easiest trap for new expat families to fall into.

## The Trap of Blind Enrollment

Local Hong Kong parents start accumulating certificates when their children are two or three years old. If you arrive in Hong Kong during your child's primary school years and try to catch up by "taking classes and getting certificates," you will find it impossible.

More importantly, Hong Kong's top international and direct subsidy schools are **long tired of cookie-cutter certificates**. They don't want to see "how many classes you took," but rather "what unique traits your child has."

## Overtaking on the Curve: "Project-Based Outcomes" over "Check-in Certificates"

For Top Talent Pass families, the most effective strategy is a **paradigm shift**—don't compete with local students on the number of certificates. Instead, present a substantial "Project-Based Outcome."

**Publishing a dedicated physical book** is the best card you can play.
