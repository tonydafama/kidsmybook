## Why tell an international-school interview with an ISBN book?

Because the interviewer can follow it. The book is a finished interest: the child took part, university professor review can be part of it, and the publishing arrangements are formal. ## How can the child tell the book in English?

Four sentences, in the child's own English: what the interest is, one page they noticed, one change they made, and what they still want to continue. Do not memorise a speech an adult wrote. If the interviewer follows up, point at the same page. The book can be Chinese, English, or bilingual. Language is chosen in consultation.

The four lines below are a shape, not a script to recite, and they do not produce a school offer.

- The book is about one interest I kept doing.
- On this page I noticed something specific.
- I changed this part after I looked again.
- I still want to continue this interest.

## Can an international school and a local school share one book?

Yes, and they should. The international interview uses English for the project and the curiosity. A local, private, or direct-subsidy interview more often uses Chinese for how the family finished the work together. Two tellings, one story.
