## What can you still do in door-knocking or interview season?

Narrow the file. Do not rush a new book.

- Pull one existing piece the child can explain and put it first.
- Move certificates and worksheets to the appendix. Do not narrate every sheet.
- Practise one telling: where the interest came from, what was done, and what changed.
- If the school asked for drawings, bring those drawings. An unfinished book does not replace them: https://kidsmybook.com/blog/mei-shu-zuo-pin-ji-vs-isbn-chu-ban .

Start when the next application cycle still has about a year. The book adds one finished outcome. It does not repair this week’s interview. ## Does a last-minute keepsake print help?

It can be a family keepsake. It is usually not mentor-led publishing. A self-funded print run may not include a process the child finished, professor review, ISBN arrangements, or portfolio packaging.
