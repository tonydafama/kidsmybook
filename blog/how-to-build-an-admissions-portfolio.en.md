Many parents feel the admissions file is thin: too short, too much like the class next door, or impossible for the child to explain. Making it stronger is not making it thicker. Put one finished story first and treat the rest as an appendix. Hub: https://kidsmybook.com/admissions-portfolio .

## How do you tell that a portfolio is too thin?

Thin usually means an interviewer cannot follow what the child did, not that the page count is low. The usual file has many certificate copies, unfinished tutoring essays, and drawings or awards with no sentence in the child’s own words. If every page is only a heading a parent wrote, the file is still thin.

A few minutes in a primary interview: https://kidsmybook.com/blog/primary-interview-how-to-use-a-book . Secondary, DSS, and direct subsidy look for a sustained interest: https://kidsmybook.com/blog/secondary-dss-interview-published-book .

## How should a school portfolio be ordered?

Put one finished outcome the child can explain at the front. Certificates, grade exams, and class exercises go behind it. On the day, open that first page. Do not narrate every award.

- After the cover: the name of one outcome, and one sentence the child can say.
- In the middle: the process, such as one observation, one revision, or one drawing that matches the page.
- At the back: certificates, tutoring work, and other classes, one or two pages each.
- Practise one telling. International and local interviews can use two tellings, not two stories: https://kidsmybook.com/blog/international-vs-local-school-interview-portfolio .

## Where do certificates and tutoring work go?

A certificate class proves enrolment and a grade. Keep it in the appendix: https://kidsmybook.com/blog/certificate-course-vs-isbn-publishing . Tutoring essays, worksheets, and mock scripts are practice, not a finished publication: https://kidsmybook.com/blog/tutoring-work-vs-published-portfolio . Both can stay. They should not occupy the first page. When drawings are enough, and when a book is the different piece: https://kidsmybook.com/blog/art-portfolio-vs-isbn-published-book .

## When is an ISBN book the difference, rather than another certificate?

Another weekend class usually adds one more sheet that looks like the family before you. A formal ISBN book the child took part in, with university professor review when it fits, is different evidence: one interest finished, and a page someone can check. Kidsmybook does not ask families to quit classes. An interest already underway can be the start of a book: https://kidsmybook.com/interest-to-published-book . A print-shop keepsake is a different path: https://kidsmybook.com/blog/self-publishing-vs-mentored-publishing .

## What can you still do if the interview is soon?

You can narrow the file you already have and choose one existing piece the child can explain. You cannot finish a 12-month publishing programme in a few days.
