Parents who search for an art portfolio usually want to know whether the interview wants a sketchbook or a formally published book. Both can sit in one file. They do not prove the same thing. The school portfolio hub explains how one book is used: https://kidsmybook.com/admissions-portfolio .

## What is the difference between an art portfolio and an ISBN book?

An art portfolio is usually a sketchbook, drawings, photos of making, or a selection from class. It shows practice. An ISBN book is a finished publication: a topic, steps the child took part in, university professor review when it fits, and book-number arrangements. In a few minutes, a loose stack of drawings can read as “my child takes art”. A book opened to one page is easier to remember as one story.

A certificate class ends with a grade sheet. Tutoring ends with practice. An art class ends with drawings. Those are three kinds of evidence. ## When is a drawing portfolio enough?

If the school asks for original work, a sketchbook, or a set number of drawings, follow that request. Drawings are the evidence when the day is about mark-making, unfinished studies, or a visual-arts direction. Kidsmybook does not ask a family to quit art class, and a book does not replace drawings the school required.

The only named public case is Hilary: age 8 at publication, a bilingual butterfly guide from watching butterflies, then a launch and a media record. That is a publishing example, not an offer from any art class or school: https://kidsmybook.com/case-studies/hilary-butterfly-guide .

## When does a published book help the interview?

If the file is only drawings and certificates, the interview is only “I drew”. A book helps when the family needs to show that one interest was finished, and the child can point at a page and say what they noticed and changed. ## Can drawings and a book go in together?

Yes. Put the book first and the drawing selection behind it. In the interview, open one page, then use one or two drawings to answer how that page was made. ## Does publication guarantee a place?

No. Kidsmybook is a publishing studio, not an admissions agent. There is no school partnership and no guaranteed place at any primary, secondary, or international school. The school decides. A book can give the portfolio one finished story. It does not replace the school’s criteria, and it does not replace drawings the school asked for.
