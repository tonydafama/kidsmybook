The question kindergarten parents ask first is whether it is too early. Most K1–K3 children are not ready to write an adult essay. A book can still be a concrete object in a portfolio if the depth matches the age and the voice stays the child’s.

## When is it not too early?

The Kidsmybook programme is roughly ages 3 to 18 and is led by interest. At kindergarten, the usual materials are drawings, observation, a story the child tells, and one thing they will actually stop to look at — insects, the kitchen, vehicles, weather. Fit depends on whether the interest is steady, and whether the family wants a keepsake or an interview portfolio.

## How is a kindergarten book different from a primary book?

A primary interview can talk about research steps and revision. A kindergarten portfolio cares more about whether the child can point at a page and say, in their own words, “I drew this” or “I saw this”. Page count, sentence length, and information density stay small. It should not become a parent brochure. Chinese, English, or bilingual follows the target school and is decided in consultation.

## How do you keep the child’s voice?

Mentors and parents set the depth. They do not assign an adult essay. The child can dictate while an adult writes down what was actually said. Drawings and observation notes carry the book. Edits ask “what do you want the reader to know?”, not “what sounds impressive?”. If a parent wrote the whole book, the first interview question gives it away.

## How can the book be used in an interview?

The book is not another certificate that looks like everyone else’s. It gives the family a specific story: where the interest came from, what the child noticed, and which page the child decided. The named public case, Hilary, was eight at publication — primary age, not a kindergarten example. A kindergarten family should judge the depth this age can honestly do, not copy an older child’s finished book.

## When should you not push it?

It may not fit if the interest is still unsteady, the child resists as soon as it is mentioned, or the family only wants a decoration that looks advanced. The studio does not guarantee a place at any kindergarten or primary school. The school decides.
