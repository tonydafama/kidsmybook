Frequently Asked Questions

What age is MyBook suitable for?

We work with children aged 6-14. The concept is always led by the child's own interest.

How long does the whole project take?

Typically 6-12 months from concept to printed book, depending on complexity.

Is the book in English or Chinese?

We support English, Traditional Chinese, or bilingual editions.

What is the HKD 500 deposit for?

It secures your Discovery Session and is fully credited toward your project fee if you proceed.

Will my child's name or photo appear publicly?

Never without your explicit written consent. Privacy is our core commitment.

What will my child actually do in this journey?

Your child drives the story idea and creative direction. Our team supports research, editing, design, and production.
